Director's Notes

Five Things We Learned

The numbers tell a story. Here's what the data actually says about how recommendation algorithms behave on a small, dense, real-world dataset.

🏆
Hybrid wins clearly — ranking loss + content features
LightFM with WARP loss directly optimises ranking rather than rating prediction. The 19-genre item features add signal that pure collaborative methods lack. Result: +0.0096 NDCG@10 over the next best model.
⚡
ALS is the best pure CF model — and 24× faster than Hybrid
Implicit-feedback matrix factorisation handles the 7.8% matrix density far better than neighbourhood methods. Train time: 0.7s. NDCG@10: 0.1070. Outstanding efficiency frontier.
🧠
NCF underperforms despite being most complex
MovieLens 100K is too small for NeuMF. The tuner landed all hyperparameters at lower bounds — the model overfits at any reasonable capacity. Deep learning needs more data before it earns its complexity.
📉
Every model fails on long-tail items
Tail NDCG@10 is near zero across the board. All models learn to recommend popular items regardless of architecture. NCF scores exactly 0.0000 on torso and tail buckets.
🚫
39% of test users are completely unserved
No model found a relevant item in the top-10 for 368 out of 943 test users. This ceiling matters more than rank-order differences between models — it's a hard wall on achievable utility.
🧊
ALS handles cold users most gracefully
In the user-activity breakdown, ALS shows the smallest degradation from active → cold users. UserCF is most sensitive to cold-start, with the steepest NDCG@10 drop across tiers.
